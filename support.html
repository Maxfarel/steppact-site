Account & sign-in

StepPact uses your email and a one-time code to sign in. If the code doesn't arrive, check your spam folder and request a new one after a minute.

Steps not counting

  • Allow StepPact to read your step data when asked.
  • Carry your iPhone while you walk.
  • Steps entered by hand are not counted.
  • Open the app daily so your progress syncs.

Payments & refunds

  • Entry fees go 100% into the challenge pool.
  • Leave before a challenge starts: entry fee back to your balance; the service fee isn't refunded.
  • Leave after it starts: the entry fee isn't refunded.
  • If nobody finishes, all entry fees are returned.
  • If a challenge is cancelled by StepPact, you get a full refund.

Delete your account

  1. In the app, go to Profile → Delete account.
  2. Your account is frozen and your balance is paid out.
  3. Challenges that haven't started are refunded. For active ones, you can wait until they end or leave and lose that entry fee.
  4. After payout, your personal data is deleted. Some transaction records are kept in anonymized form where the law requires it.

Can't access the app? Email us from the address on your account.

Report a problem

Found a bug, unfair play, or inappropriate content? Use the report button in the app or email us with screenshots.
