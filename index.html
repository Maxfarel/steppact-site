How it works

Four steps.
Every day.

Motivation fades. A commitment doesn't. StepPact gives your daily walk a deadline, a group, and something on the line.

01

Pick a challenge

Every challenge has a daily step target and a set number of days. Join a public one or start a private challenge with friends.

02

Commit

Pay the entry fee before the start. You see the full cost, including the service fee, before you confirm. Free challenges exist too.

03

Walk

Your steps are read automatically from your iPhone. Steps typed in by hand don't count, so every result is real.

04

Finish

Hit the target every day. You can miss one day and still finish. The pool is split equally among everyone who finished.

Rules in plain English

No fine print.
No surprises.

Every entry fee goes into the pool. A separate service fee is shown on top, before you pay.

Example · $20 challenge, paid by card
Entry fee100% goes to the pool $20.00
Service fee5% + $0.50 by card · 5% from balance $1.50
Total $21.50
If nobody finishes, every entry fee is returned.
  • Entry feesFrom $10 to $500 for paid challenges. Free challenges cost nothing.
  • FinishingReach the daily target on every day of the challenge. One missed day is allowed.
  • PayoutsThe pool is split equally among finishers and credited to your StepPact balance.
  • Leaving earlyBefore the start: entry fee back to your balance. After the start: the entry fee is not refunded.
  • CancellationsIf a challenge is cancelled by StepPact, you get a full refund, including the service fee.
  • LimitsUp to 5 paid challenges at a time.
You can lose your entry fee if you don't finish. Only join with an amount you're comfortable losing. StepPact is about building a habit, not making money.

FAQ

Questions,
answered.

How are my steps counted?

StepPact reads your steps automatically from your iPhone. You don't need to log anything. Steps entered by hand are not counted.

What if I miss a day?

Every challenge allows one missed day. Miss two or more and you won't finish that challenge.

How is the pool split?

All entry fees go into the pool. When the challenge ends, the pool is divided equally among everyone who finished. If nobody finishes, all entry fees are returned.

Is StepPact available in my state?

Free challenges are available across the US. Paid challenges are only available in eligible states, and the app checks your location before you join one.

Who can use StepPact?

You must be 18 or older to use StepPact.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. Go to Profile and request account deletion. Your balance is paid out first, then your personal data is deleted. Learn more.
